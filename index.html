The MW Tailleur Philosophy

The Art of Bespoke Wedding Tailoring

At MW Tailleur, we believe a wedding suit should be as individual as the gentleman wearing it.

Our approach combines personalised styling, considered fabric selection and made-to-measure tailoring to create garments that reflect your character and the significance of your wedding day.

Whether you prefer timeless simplicity, distinctive jacquard detailing or the finest Italian fabrics, our collection offers an opportunity to create a look that feels entirely your own.

    Fabrics & Craftsmanship

    Why Fabric Matters

    The fabric you choose shapes how a suit looks, feels and hangs, and it is the main reason prices differ between collections.

    Plain Wool

    Solid, understated cloth for a clean, timeless silhouette. A versatile foundation for a groom who values simplicity, and the entry point to the collection.

    Premium & Jacquard

    Richer textures and woven decorative patterns for a more expressive look. Jacquard cloth carries its pattern within the weave itself, giving depth and character in close-up.

    Italian Luxury

    A selection from respected textile mills, for clients who want exceptional quality, refined texture and a distinctive finish. See the fabric houses listed in the Italian Luxury collection above. Availability varies by colour, pattern and weight.

    Made around you

    Every suit is tailored to your measurements and specified to your taste. Options include:

    • Fit and fit adjustments
    • Lapel style and width
    • Button configuration
    • Pockets and vents
    • Sleeve details
    • Lining and shoulder construction
    • Trouser styling and waistbands
    • Monograms and embroidery

    Construction options are confirmed at your consultation.
